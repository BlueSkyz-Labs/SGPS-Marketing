Vững Tay Lái is a BlueSkyz Labs product for people studying the class-B driving theory test in Vietnam. The project's goal is to help learners understand why an answer is right, see where they still misunderstand, and go back to exactly that point, rather than memorising answers. This is a demo, still being finished. This post walks through real screens of the development build.

## An evening of revision (an illustrative scenario)

The scenario below uses sample answers to show how the product works. The learner is not a real user.

After work, a learner opens Vững Tay Lái. The **Today** screen offers one task for the stage the learner chose, with shortcuts to the mock exam, road signs and practical driving.

The first question is a **critical question** about alcohol and driving home. The app labels it as a critical question and does not show the correct answer yet.

The learner picks answer A. Before they learn whether it is right, the app asks: **how sure are you?** There are four levels, and this time they choose "Tạm chắc" (fairly sure).

The result appears: **not correct**. Below it, "Hiểu nhanh" gives a short explanation, labelled as written by Vững Tay Lái rather than the legal text. Because the learner chose "fairly sure", the note that comes with it reads "Chưa chắc và cần ôn lại" (not sure and needs review).

To see the basis, the learner opens **Nguồn tham chiếu** (reference sources). Legal sources are listed apart from the study content, each with its issuing authority, effective dates and the date the source was last checked.

At the end of the evening, the **Journey** screen shows them at the "Học & luyện tập" (learn and practise) stage, with finished stages checked. The path is stored on the device; the demo has no accounts.

## Highlights

- **Confidence first, answer second.** Learners record how sure they are before they see the result, so they notice the points they thought they understood.
- **Explanations with sources.** Vững Tay Lái's own explanations are kept apart from the legal texts, and each source shows its effective dates and when it was last checked.
- **One task for today.** The Today screen offers one task that fits the learner's stage, with shortcuts to the mock exam, road signs and practical driving.
- **No accounts yet.** The learning path is stored on the learner's device.

## Where it stands

- Vững Tay Lái is a web demo, still being finished, and not yet open to users.
- Android and iOS apps are on the development roadmap and are not in the app stores.
- Vững Tay Lái is not an official government app. The road signs and questions in the screenshots do not imply any affiliation with an authority.
- The screenshots were captured on 25 October 2026 from a development build with sample answers.
