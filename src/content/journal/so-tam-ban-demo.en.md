Sổ Tâm is a BlueSkyz Labs product: a private journal for writing down your thoughts, looking back and keeping what matters. The project's goal is a quiet place to write, with no pace to keep up with and nothing you have to do. This is a demo, still being finished. This post walks through real screens of the development build, captured on 25 October 2026.

## A Sunday evening (an illustrative scenario)

The scenario below uses sample text to show how the product works. The person in it is not a real user.

**Starting.** Someone opens Sổ Tâm in their phone's browser for the first time. The welcome card reads "Giữ điều có ý nghĩa." (keep what matters) and says plainly that no account is needed, and that on the web the writing stays in this browser, unencrypted and not synced. They tap **Bắt đầu** (start).

**Writing a page.** The **Today** page shows the date, "Chủ Nhật, 25 tháng 10" (Sunday, 25 October), with the solar term of the year: that day falls in Sương Giáng (frost's descent). They write a few lines in the writing area. Below it, **Đã lưu trên thiết bị** (saved on this device) appears with a word count.

**Naming a feeling, if they want.** The question "Hôm nay bạn thế nào?" (how are you today?) is marked optional. Moods are words such as Nặng lòng (heavy-hearted), Xao động (unsettled), Khó gọi tên (hard to name), Yên (calm) and Nhẹ lòng (light-hearted); this time they pick "Nhẹ lòng". The **touchpoints** are words too, each with a small ink mark: family, work, body and mind, gratitude, faith, nature and rest. They pick "Tri ân" (gratitude).

**Looking back.** The **Journal** tab shows the unfinished draft with a "tiếp →" (continue) link. The **Memories** tab is still empty, with a brush-stroke circle and a note that finished pages will be kept in the Journal, by month, on this device. The app labels both tabs as previews.

**The bell.** In **Settings**, the mindful bell is on. The app states that the bell is generated entirely in the browser, downloads no file and sends nothing over the network.

## Highlights

- **No account to start.** Open it and write; the app says where web writing is stored.
- **Every choice is optional.** There is no pace to keep up with; mood and touchpoints are there only if you want them.
- **Feelings in words.** Moods and touchpoints are words, not a score.
- **A calendar with solar terms.** The Today page shows the date with the solar term of the year.
- **A bell that sends nothing.** The bell is generated in the browser.

## Where it stands

- Sổ Tâm is in development. The web version is a demo, still being finished.
- On the web, writing is stored in the browser, not yet encrypted or synced. Journal and Memories are previews.
- Android and iOS apps are in development and are not in the app stores.
- The screenshots were captured on 25 October 2026 from a development build with sample text, not anyone's real journal.

See the screenshots and product details on the [Sổ Tâm page](/en/products/sotam/). News about the product will appear under Product updates.
