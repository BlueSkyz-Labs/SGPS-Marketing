Sổ Tâm is a BlueSkyz Labs product: a private journal for writing down your thoughts, looking back and keeping what matters. The project's goal is a place to keep what matters, with no pace to keep up with and nothing you have to do. This is a demo, still being finished. This post walks through real screens of the development build, captured on 25 October 2026.

## A Sunday (an illustrative scenario)

The scenario below uses sample text to illustrate what the screens show. The person in it is not a real user.

**Starting.** Someone opens Sổ Tâm in their phone's browser for the first time. The welcome card reads "Giữ điều có ý nghĩa." (keep what matters) and says plainly that no account is needed, and that on the web the writing stays in this browser, unencrypted and does not sync by itself. They tap **Bắt đầu** (start).

**Writing a page.** The **Hôm nay** (Today) page shows the date, "Chủ Nhật, 25 tháng 10" (Sunday, 25 October), with that day's solar term: that day falls in Sương Giáng (frost's descent). They write a few lines in the writing area. Below it, **Đã lưu trên thiết bị** (saved on this device) appears with a word count.

**Naming a feeling, if they want.** The question "Hôm nay bạn thế nào?" (how are you today?) is marked optional. Moods are words such as Nặng lòng (heavy-hearted), Xao động (unsettled), Khó gọi tên (hard to name), Yên (calm) and Nhẹ lòng (light-hearted); this time they pick "Nhẹ lòng". The **Điểm chạm** (touchpoints) are words too, each with a small icon: family, work, body and mind, gratitude, belief, earth and sky, and rest. They pick "Tri ân" (gratitude).

**Looking back.** The **Ghi chép** (Journal) tab shows the unfinished draft with a "tiếp →" (continue) link. The **Ký ức** (Memories) tab is still empty, with a brush-stroke circle and a note that finished pages will be kept in the Journal, by month, on this device. The app labels both tabs as previews.

**The bell.** In **Cài đặt** (Settings), the mindful bell is on. The app states that the bell is generated entirely in the browser, downloads no file and sends nothing over the network.

## Highlights

- **No account to start.** Open it and write; the app says where web writing is stored.
- **Nothing you have to do.** The mood question is marked optional, and there is no pace to keep up with.
- **Feelings in words.** Moods and touchpoints are everyday words.
- **The date with its solar term.** The Today page shows the date and that day's solar term.
- **A bell made in the browser.** The app states it downloads no file and sends nothing over the network.

## Where it stands

- Sổ Tâm is in development. The web version is a demo, still being finished.
- On the web, writing is stored in the browser, unencrypted and not synced automatically. Journal and Memories are previews.
- Android and iOS apps are in development and are not in the app stores.
- The screenshots were captured on 25 October 2026 from a development build with sample text, not anyone's real journal.

See the screenshots and product details on the [Sổ Tâm page](/en/products/sotam/). News about the product will appear under Product updates.
