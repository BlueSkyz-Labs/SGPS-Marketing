Sổ Trọ, BlueSkyz Labs' rent book, is now in web preview for landlords in Vietnam. This post covers what it does and what is available today. Open it and see who still owes rent this month and what needs doing today.

## What Sổ Trọ helps with

- **See it at a glance**: who still owes rent this month and what needs doing today.
- **Collect rent and record each payment once**, with a review-then-confirm step, a bank-transfer QR code and a receipt to copy into Zalo.
- **Keep rooms, tenants, meter readings and the residence register in one place**, with a residence spreadsheet to download.
- **Large text and big buttons** built for older landlords; a family member's phone can join just by scanning a QR code.

## Where it stands

- Sổ Trọ is in web preview and accepts new rent books in limited numbers.
- Android and iOS apps are in development and not yet in the app stores.
- The screenshots on the product page were captured on 30 September 2026 from a test build with sample data.

See the overview, screenshots and a short video on the [Sổ Trọ page](/en/products/sotro/). When a new release ships, we will post it under Product updates.
