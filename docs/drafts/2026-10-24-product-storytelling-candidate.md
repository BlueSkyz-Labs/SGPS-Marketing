# Product storytelling — copy candidate (Owner approval required)

**Status:** CANDIDATE. Not rendered anywhere. New public copy needs the Owner's content approval before it ships.
**Date:** 2026-10-24 (GMT+7)
**Scope:** Owner request of 2026-10-24: a premium, story-led presentation of the three current products (Sổ Trọ, Sổ Tâm, Vững Tay Lái) with real images and logos. Video waits for the official go-live.

## Method

Each story follows one arc: **a real person → the tension they live with → the turn the product makes → where it honestly stands today.**
Every sentence traces to a line in the product's own Product Truth or to the registered claim record on this site. The trace column names the source. A sentence with no source is not in this deck.

Writing rules applied:

- No numbers on feature lists (EX-07). No superlatives, no "best", no pass or income promises.
- Status is said plainly in each story's last beat. No app, store or sign-in link for a product that is not launched.
- EN is canonical. VI is written natively, not translated word for word. ZH and ZH-Hant follow after approval and are marked machine-assisted.
- Visuals: Sổ Trọ uses the real UI captures already in its showcase record. Sổ Tâm and Vững Tay Lái use their identity art until their real captures land (separate PRs). No staged photography, no stock people.
