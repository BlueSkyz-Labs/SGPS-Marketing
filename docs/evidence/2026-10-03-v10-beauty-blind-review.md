# v10 E6: Beauty-Blind review (BPXS §18)

**Recorded:** 2026-10-03, by the orchestrator acting as the reviewer lane. **Build reviewed:** `main@1163a78`; fix based on `main@5b76df4`, production-origin static build served locally.

**Scope:** 4 routes (`/en/`, `/vi/`, `/vi/products/sotro/`, `/en/about/`) × 390 and 1440 px × light and dark = 16 full-page captures, Chromium, `reducedMotion: reduce`. Two extra viewport captures were taken to settle a capture artifact.

**Method:**

- **Pass 1 (styling ignored):** structure, hierarchy, task clarity.
- **Pass 2:** the anti-failure tests.
  - Could this belong to any premium SaaS?
  - Is anything decorative without function (BPXS §7.3)?

**Independence:** the same agent family wrote much of the UI, so this is **not** independent review and **not** Human E4 (AGENTS rule 43). Severity is a reviewer judgement, not measured user impact.

## What passes

- **Pass 1 structure:** every route has one H1, a clear primary action, and status and limits stated before persuasion. `/en/about/` is the cleanest page: mission, the two products, "what you can check" and two actions, with nothing decorative.
- **Distinctiveness that is real:**
  - the trust-forward pattern ("Check what we say", claims with stated limits, "This site sets no cookies" linked to evidence);
  - real product UI with an explicit sample-data label;
  - Vietnamese-first product surfaces.
    These would **not** belong to a generic SaaS. BB-01 is about copy only.
- **Theme parity:** the ink hero and footer planes stay dark in light mode, which is an intentional brand plane. No contrast or legibility breaks were seen in either theme at either width.
- **Product page:** status, platform availability and the sample-data disclosure are above the fold. The phone and desktop showcases render correctly at viewport size.
  - The blank regions in full-page captures were a capture artifact: lazy images never scrolled into view. Re-verified with a viewport probe: 4/4 visible images decoded, `naturalWidth` > 0.
  - The undecoded images sit in the collapsed "more screens" group.
