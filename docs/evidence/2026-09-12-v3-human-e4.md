# v3 Human E4 Protocol — God-tier Trust Experience

**Status: NOT RUN.** No real-participant study has occurred for the v3

## Participant and session protocol

- **Participants:** 5–8 per locale (EN, VI), adults, mixed technical
  backgrounds; at least two participants unfamiliar with the site.
- **Session:** 30–45 minutes, think-aloud, one participant at a time,
  remote or in person; recorded only with consent.
- **Artifacts observed:** `/en/` (Intent Lens, Journey Bar), `/en/security/`
  (Integrity Lens, Source Trace), an Evidence Passport
  (`/en/evidence/security-reporting-is-private/`), Decision Room
  (`/en/decision-room/`), Command Navigator (provenance search), and the
  EN↔VI switch.
- **Recording rule:** observations (what happened) are recorded separately
  from interpretation (why we think it happened). Quotes are verbatim or
  omitted — never paraphrased into claims.

## Real-participant tasks

1. Explain a truth state without prompting.
2. Open "Verify this page" and identify a supporting source.
3. Explain "establishes" versus "does not establish".
4. Find and use an Evidence Passport.
5. Compare two public items in Decision Room and explain why there is no
   score or recommendation.
6. Switch EN/VI and retain evidence context.
7. Use Provenance Search to find a security or privacy evidence destination.
8. Explain whether "not published" means "does not exist".

## Success criteria

- Task 2 and 4: participant reaches the correct surface unaided in under
  90 seconds.
- Task 5: participant can state that the room compares facts and produces no
  verdict.
- Task 8: participant distinguishes "not published" from "does not exist".
- No task may depend on knowledge of internal governance vocabulary.
