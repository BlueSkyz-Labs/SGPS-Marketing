- No destructive, high-volume or credentialed actions.

**Vocabulary:** PASS / FAIL / NOT VERIFIED / NOT APPLICABLE. Severity uses the RCR scale (R1 low … R4 root). Agent walkthroughs are preflight only and are **not** Human E4.

## Self red-team of this audit (limits)

- **Egress:** the runtime probes came from one US egress, and the Access metadata shows `real_country: US`. VN-edge behaviour (country hint, locale suggestion) was not observed. It is NOT VERIFIED.
- **Engines:** the dynamic XSS and persona runs used Chromium only. Firefox and WebKit stay with CI.
- **Field data:** none. All timings are lab or emulated.
- **Subdomains:** enumeration was a guessed list, not zone data, so RT-11 cannot prove absence.
- **Personas:** the walkthroughs were written and judged by the same agent. They are preflight evidence only (AGENTS rule 43).
