## Findings and exact remediation

### H-01: Dossier can publish a claim after its provenance becomes unknown

Priority: P1. This is an observed conditional gap, not a proven live exploit.

In src/lib/dossier.ts, the claims branch checks that the claim exists but
builds an entry even when getPublicProvenance returns null or unknown. Current
tests reject missing identifiers but do not model an existing claim that
loses valid independent evidence. A later registry change could show its
statement without a trustworthy source chain.

Implement a negative fixture or dependency seam in
tests/architecture/c4-dossier-provenance.test.mjs. Prove RED for a real claim
with refused provenance. Make the smallest fix in src/lib/dossier.ts: reject
the claim with a reason, produce complete=false and retain valid canonical
labels, references and authored freshness. Cover EN, VI, zh, print and no-JS.
If the negative fixture disproves the hypothesis, record that and avoid an
unnecessary code change.

### H-02: Live work router is behind C4 implementation

Priority: P1. Inspect #224 latest head and reconcile against current main,
open PRs and actual source evidence. Keep one active IN_PROGRESS wave. Preserve
owner gates and existing evidence paths. Make stale PR references detectable
and prevent a new agent from treating a merged task as active. No second
registry, plan router or unsupported merge-completion claim.

### H-03: Concurrent C4 branches can overwrite each other

Priority: P1. Review changed files, ancestry, conflicts and exact-head CI.
Order #228 before #229, #230 and #231. Review #225 against real route helpers
and #232 against the latest dossier/provenance contract. In particular,
preserve Atelier controls and Dossier print CSS during #231 conflict repair.
Mergeable does not mean ready: require current base, required checks and review
conditions.

### H-04: Public Product Truth and contact remain owner-fact gated

Priority: P1. C2-P0 still requires an authentic product capture and approved
facts and proof. Do not manufacture marketing content to activate the public
product registry. Keep elegant honest-empty states and functional fallback
from contact to About and Security when a verified business email is absent.

Store the real asset, rights, product identifier, capability evidence,
owner approval and public route together before publication. Cloudflare
Access owner-only status and actual public release approval stay separate
from source merge.

### H-05: Multilingual and search completeness

Priority: P1. EN, VI and Simplified Chinese zh-Hans are live language routes.
Traditional Chinese zh-Hant is architecture-ready, not published runtime
parity. When #225 or other routes merge, derive truthful canonical URLs,

Do not advertise unavailable routes. Test CJK typography, date semantics,

### H-06: Optical and accessibility consistency

Priority: P1. Audit home, product-empty, Decision Room, Dossier, print,
Editions, privacy, security and contact in Light and Dark at 320, 390, 768
and 1440 pixels. Verify meaningful keyboard order, visible focus, accessible
status and reset, 44px targets, reduced motion, forced colors, text-spacing
overrides, zoom, CJK lines and no-JS content. Record baseline, target and
actual visual evidence. Automated browsers do not satisfy human E4.

### H-07: Measurement and performance must be truthful

Priority: P2. src/lib/analytics.ts emits local CustomEvents only; network
transmission is OFF. No conversion uplift or production RUM claim. A future
provider needs a privacy, minimization, retention and owner decision first.
Measure route-level JS, image and font behavior, CLS and Lighthouse. Fix
root causes before increasing the existing client budget.

### H-08: Public projection and security attack chains

### H-09: Production recovery is distinct from CI

Priority: P2. After a merge, read back deployed revision, host, status,
