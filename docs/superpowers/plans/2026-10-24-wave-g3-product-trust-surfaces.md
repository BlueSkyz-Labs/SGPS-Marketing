### G3-A — lead decisions (lane A, product surfaces: G-04, G-05, G-06)

- Branch `claude/marketing-project-audit-623e4t-wave-g3a`, worktree `scratchpad/wt/g3a`, base = the G3 base commit on top of G2 `5110f51` (so it carries G1 + G2; it lands after #678).
- G-04 `.profile-does__tile` → no border, radius, background, inset or shadow. Each `li` is a row with `border-top: 1px solid var(--c4-hairline)` and `padding: var(--space-3) 0`; the list keeps its grid (one column below 48rem, two columns from 48rem with `column-gap: var(--space-6)`), so each row rules its own column. Text at `--c4-type-reading`, `--text-primary`, `max-width: var(--c4-reading-measure)`. No numeral column: EX-07 (wave E, PR #670) keeps feature numerals out because the story chapters already carry "01 / 05" on the same page, and two unrelated 01–05 sequences read as one. The list is hairline rows only; the visible strings do not change. DOM hooks (`data-what-it-does`, `#profile-jobs`, class names) stay.
- G-05 inspect links: `font-size: var(--c4-type-meta)`, `font-weight: 500`, `color: var(--text-secondary)`, `text-decoration-color: color-mix(in srgb, currentColor 45%, transparent)`, `text-underline-offset: 0.2em`; on `:hover` and `:focus-visible` the colour is `--text-primary` and the underline is full. Keep `min-height: 2.75rem` (tap target), `target`, `rel`, `data-capture-inspect`, the `↗` glyph and the `aria-hidden` span. Text unchanged. Both rule sets (FeatureStory `<style>` and ProductShowcase `<style is:inline>` for `.showcase__inspect`) move together; one shared rule in `c4-quiet-authority.css` under `:where()` is preferred over two copies.
- G-06 `ProductCard` on `/products/` only: add an opt-in `variant="ledger"` prop (default keeps today's card for `ProductHouse` on the home page). In `ledger` the article drops `rounded-*`, `border`, `bg-[var(--surface-raised)]`, the padding box and the hover shadow; it becomes a row with `border-top: 1px solid var(--c4-hairline)`, `padding: var(--space-6) 0`, same inner grid; the title gains the G1 journal hover (`--text-primary`, underline on `:hover`/`:focus-visible`). The last row keeps a closing hairline (`border-bottom`) so the list reads as a ledger. All `data-product-card`, status badge and thumb hooks stay. Four locale index pages pass the prop.
- Contrast: `--text-secondary` (#334155 light, #cbd5e1 dark) on the page surfaces must stay ≥ 4.5:1 for the meta links; axe on the touched routes in both themes is the proof.

### G3-B — lead decisions (lane B, trust surfaces: G-07, G-08, G-09)
