## Jobs and real environments (hypothetical, not observed)
J1 a first-time mobile visitor with five minutes and unreliable connection asks whether any product addresses their concrete task; J2 a buyer must explain publicly checkable status to a colleague; J3 a non-technical family user does not know AI/product terminology; J4 a returning evaluator needs to know whether an earlier assumption remains valid; J5 a user of keyboard/screen reader/text zoom or a low-end in-app browser needs the SAME factual decision; J6 a privacy-conscious visitor does not want profiling or forced contact; J7 a visitor on a dead, private, or unavailable product link needs a safe route back. The need for these improvements must be validated, not presumed.
