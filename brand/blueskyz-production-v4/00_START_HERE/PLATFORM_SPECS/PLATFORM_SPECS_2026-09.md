# Platform Asset Specifications - Reviewed 2026-09-07

This file records the production assumptions used by Brand Kit v4. Always re-check platform help pages before major campaigns because platform specifications can change.

## YouTube
- Recommended banner: **2560x1440 px**.
- Minimum upload: **2048x1152 px**, 16:9.
- Official safe area at minimum upload: **1235x338 px**; the v4 2560x1440 banner keeps critical content within a conservative centered safe zone.
- Banner file size: 6 MB or less.
- Official reference: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525

## LinkedIn
- Personal profile cover: **1584x396 px** recommended.
- Company Page cover: **1512x256 px** recommended as of this release.
- Company Page logo: **400x400 px** recommended.
- Official references: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a568217 and https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563309

## Other channels
The kit includes conservative, widely supported production sizes for X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Open Graph, email, presentation and video. Where a platform auto-crops by device, keep critical logos/text away from outer edges and review the crop after upload.
