## Source-of-truth hierarchy
1. `02_LOGOS/01_VECTOR_SVG/FLAT_COLOR` and `FULL_LOCKUPS` - canonical scalable flat identity.
2. `02_LOGOS/02_PNG_TRANSPARENT` - approved Prismatic Hero raster assets for premium/campaign use.
3. `03_ICONS` - favicon/PWA, brand-principle and product icons.
4. `04_DIGITAL` - exact-size channel assets.
5. `05_CORPORATE` - standard office/print templates; vendor-proof before large-volume or specialist finishing.
6. `06_PRODUCT_BRANDS/01_LOCKUPS_SVG` - endorsed product lockups.
7. `10_CONCEPT_REFERENCE` and `08_REFERENCE_MOCKUPS` - never source-of-truth.

## Identity
The R4d Core Mark is the canonical everyday symbol. The Prismatic Hero expression is a premium dimensional rendering and must not be used where raster scaling or reproduction quality is uncertain.

### Brand line
**Intelligence. Elevated. Impact.**

### Principles
- Intelligence - Deep thinking. Smart solutions.
- Elevation - Better perspective. Greater impact.
- Trust - Reliable, secure, consistent.
- Impact - Real value. Real change.

## Logo rules
- Clearspace: minimum 1x, where x is the cap height of the B in BLUESKYZ.
- Full lockup minimum digital width: 160 px. Micro/Core Mark minimum digital width: 32 px.
- Full lockup minimum print width: 20 mm. Core Mark: 6 mm.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor outside approved variants, add effects, or place on low-contrast/noisy fields.
- Use flat vectors for UI, small sizes, engraving, embroidery planning and infinite scaling. Use Prismatic Hero PNGs for hero/campaign moments.

## Color/accessibility
